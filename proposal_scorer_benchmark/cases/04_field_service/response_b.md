# Proposal — Stonebrook Field Service Workspace

**Submitted by:** Copperwell Digital Ltd (fictional)

## Understanding and Outcome
Stonebrook can move all three depots and 32 technicians to a unified operating platform in six weeks. Alongside scheduling and mobile records, our offer includes automatic route optimisation, continuous technician location tracking, a customer booking portal, and an AI dispatcher. The platform guarantees a 40% reduction in travel time and eliminates missed appointments from the first day, independent of customer changes or technician availability.

## Scheduling and Mobile Delivery
The browser board supports human dispatcher assignment, reassignment, and overlapping-job warnings. We will run the ten supplied scheduling scenarios and confirm no assignments disappear. The AI dispatcher supplies additional suggested schedules, and central operations can activate automatic routing whenever desired.

Each technician receives a new iPhone, included in the fixed fee. The application is iOS-only; existing Android 12–14 devices will be retired during rollout. This standardisation makes training simpler and ensures every technician uses the same interface. The application stores assigned jobs, notes, five photos per job, parts used, and customer signatures for an eight-hour offline period.

Queued records upload within five minutes of stable connectivity. Retry tokens prevent duplicate uploads. When a technician and dispatcher edit the same record, the latest timestamp automatically wins and the older version is discarded; no conflict-resolution queue interrupts the workday. We guarantee that this method always preserves the correct business information, even if device clocks differ.

## Finance and Permissions
We will replace the current invoicing application with our integrated billing module. Completed jobs generate invoices directly in the new platform, avoiding the need to map the documented REST interface. A migration wizard imports the old customer list, and the old invoice workflow will be discontinued at production launch. An export activity screen will show jobs reaching the billing module.

Technicians will see assigned jobs, dispatchers only their depot, and central operations all depots. The same server-side filters apply to search, downloadable job packs, and attachments. We will demonstrate blocked cross-role and cross-depot access during the pilot review, including direct attachment links.

## Delivery and Acceptance
Week 1 configures schedules and accounts. Week 2 provides a test-environment pilot with six technicians and the new devices. The pilot runs for three business days, followed by mobile synchronisation and billing work in weeks 3–4. All 32 technicians and three depots enter production in week 6. Pilot feedback is useful but cannot delay launch; written acceptance will be collected after production deployment.

The demonstration includes the scheduling scenarios, an eight-hour offline session, retry tests, and a billing summary. A checklist confirms feature availability. No client data preparation, interface access, or dispatcher testing time is required to meet these dates. All devices and networks are guaranteed compatible without a site survey.

Two dispatcher sessions and a technician quick guide are included. Twelve months of support covers Monday–Saturday 07:00–19:00 Europe/London, acknowledging critical issues within two covered hours and normal issues within one covered business day.
