# Proposal — LindenVale Staff Training Portal

**Submitted by:** Elmbridge Software Studio (fictional)

## Understanding and Outcome
LindenVale's store teams need a consistent place to find assigned training and record progress. Today, separate spreadsheets make it difficult for central trainers to see who has completed a course. We propose a browser-based learning portal for the 18 stores and 480 employees, with a straightforward home page for staff and a reporting area for managers. LindenVale will continue to supply the German and English learning content.

The initial discovery workshop will confirm terminology, reporting preferences, and administrator responsibilities. Its output will be a prioritised backlog and a collection of screen designs for review. We will use short demonstrations during development so the project team can see progress and provide feedback before rollout.

## Learning and Administration
The portal will use Microsoft Entra ID for sign-in. Employees will have personal learning pages, store managers will have reporting pages, and central trainers will have administration pages. The precise permissions for reports and downloads will be agreed during configuration. We expect this approach to support routine store management without giving every user the same interface.

We will import the ten supplied SCORM 1.2 courses and show completion status in the learning queue. A content workshop will establish any package-specific behaviour. Course assignments, due dates, and employee profiles will be available to administrators. We will demonstrate a representative course journey before the client reviews the wider catalogue.

Employee records will be loaded from a CSV provided by LindenVale. The import routine will check for missing identifiers and report rejected rows. Scheduling the import and handling staff changes will be agreed with the HR team during discovery, alongside the CSV layout and file-transfer arrangements. We assume sample data will be available early enough to inform development.

## Reporting and Communication
Store dashboards will display assigned, overdue, and completed counts, with a central summary and CSV export. We will match the counts and export rows exactly against LindenVale's approved 40-record reference dataset as part of user testing. The proposed report layout includes filters for store, course, and completion status, helping managers focus on training that needs attention.

Email reminders will draw attention to approaching deadlines and outstanding courses. Administrators will be able to review message wording in a configuration session. The sending schedule and suppression rules will be established once the client has reviewed the employee communication policy. This also gives store managers an opportunity to agree a practical approach to follow-up.

## Delivery and Commercial Approach
Delivery proceeds through discovery, configuration, reporting, user testing, and rollout. We recommend starting with two stores and then introducing further stores in groups. We will agree milestone dates and the pilot review period once the backlog has been approved. Weekly project calls and a shared action log are included in our working approach.

The indicative implementation allowance is €46,000 excluding VAT. Hosting, software licences, and post-launch support will be quoted after platform selection. We expect a small management group to coordinate decisions, but its time commitment and any effects on price remain to be discussed.

We will provide an administrator handover and an operating guide. Support will be available through an email helpdesk, with a service package selected before launch. User testing will be coordinated with the nominated client lead, who will collate feedback and help prioritise corrections. The final delivery checklist and acceptance arrangements will be agreed as the project develops.
