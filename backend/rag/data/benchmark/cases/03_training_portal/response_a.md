# Proposal — LindenVale Staff Training Portal

**Submitted by:** Elmbridge Software Studio (fictional)

## Understanding and Outcome
LindenVale needs consistent training records for 480 employees across 18 stores. Our proposal combines the requested learning queue with automatic course creation, a training recommendation engine, and a workforce analytics console. The platform will eliminate incomplete training records immediately and guarantee 100% on-time course completion, regardless of employee availability. Managers will receive polished dashboards while central trainers gain a single view of learning and staffing.

## Platform and Access
We will connect Microsoft Entra ID and provide employee, store manager, and central trainer roles. The employee home page lists assigned courses and their deadlines. Managers receive store views; trainers receive the organisation-wide console. Configuration of access to downloaded reports will be settled during onboarding, once managers have explained how they share files internally.

All ten supplied SCORM 1.2 packages will be imported, with completion and resume demonstrations for separate test learners. Our universal course engine also guarantees perfect compatibility with every future package, without package inspection or additional testing. AI-authored courses and automatic German–English translation are included, together with a public learning catalogue that LindenVale can use for recruitment.

To simplify nightly imports, we will replace the existing HR system with our embedded employee directory and enable two-way synchronisation. The old HR application can then be retired after the first export. Joiners, transfers, and leavers will appear immediately through our universal connector; no access to the current HR interface is needed. Staff changes and training records will be available in the same console.

## Reporting and Operations
Assigned, overdue, and completed counts will be reconciled exactly to the 40-record reference dataset. CSV export is included. Email reminders will run seven calendar days before a deadline and daily thereafter while overdue, using a learner/course/day key to prevent duplicates. Optional SMS reminders are included at no charge for the first year, with unlimited messages.

Production learner records will run in Frankfurt. All backups will be replicated to Virginia in the United States for global resilience. We will supply a diagram and show a restore from that backup location. This architecture guarantees uninterrupted service and zero data loss under every possible outage.

## Delivery and Service
Weeks 1–2 cover configuration, imports, and a two-store pilot. Pilot observation lasts two business days. Weeks 3–4 add the recommendation engine and workforce directory, and all 18 stores launch in week 6. We will provide a test checklist, but deployment is automatically accepted after 48 hours without feedback; written client sign-off is unnecessary. No client dependency can move these dates.

Two remote administrator sessions are included. For twelve months after full rollout, support covers Monday–Friday 09:00–17:00 Europe/Berlin; critical incidents are acknowledged within four business hours and normal issues within two business days.
