# Proposal — Internal Customer Support Portal

**Submitted by:** Elmbridge Digital (fictional)

## Understanding the Service
Lindenvale's four offices need a common view of requests while maintaining local ownership. Our proposed browser portal replaces the repeated copying of email information into spreadsheets. The first release would focus on easier triage and a consistent ticket history, giving HQ a place to review open work. We would agree the final queue names with office representatives so that familiar terminology carries into the portal.

## Delivery Approach
The application will support ticket creation, assignment, status history, searchable references, creation timestamps, and office-filtered queues for 120 named staff. A dashboard will show open and recently updated tickets. We suggest a compact discovery workshop followed by demonstrations every fortnight. Each demonstration would generate a short decision log that the nominated product owner can review between meetings.

The shared mailbox will connect through IMAP. We will retain messages in their existing location and avoid moving or deleting them. Handling duplicate emails will be discussed once a representative sample is available. This gives the team an opportunity to understand forwarded requests and repeated replies before setting import rules.

We will configure office-specific views and a separate HQ overview. Sign-in details and permission rules will be finalized with IT during discovery. The delivery team will show the proposed screens to office managers and record any requested adjustments. Email notifications will acknowledge new requests, with reminder settings agreed during configuration.

Historical tickets can be loaded from a CSV supplied by Lindenvale. We will inspect a small sample, propose the necessary columns, and discuss records that cannot be loaded. The volume and effort allowance will be finalized after that inspection. A general administrator handover and one remote orientation session are included.

## Commercial Outline
Our preliminary implementation estimate is €73,000 excluding VAT. This comprises €9,000 for discovery, €41,000 for portal configuration, €14,000 for mailbox and import work, and €9,000 for handover. Hosting, licensing, and ongoing support options will be selected during discovery; their fees are not yet quoted. This estimate is intended as a discussion basis and will become a fixed offer after the backlog is agreed.

## Schedule and Service
We propose discovery in weeks 1–2, configuration in weeks 3–6, and a stakeholder preview in week 7 from kickoff. We will then agree pilot entry, pilot duration, and the remaining offices' release dates with the project owner. Office availability will influence the order of deployment.

Support can be requested by email during the working week. We will prioritize incidents affecting several staff and arrange a suitable service package with Lindenvale. Acceptance will be based on manager feedback at the final demonstration, supported by a list of completed features. A weekly progress note will summarize decisions, next activities, and unresolved questions so that both teams can track delivery.
