# Internal Delivery and Compliance Policy

## Data residency

Production customer data must remain in European Union regions. Primary production records may be hosted in Frankfurt. Encrypted backups may be stored in Frankfurt or Dublin. Customer records and backups must not be replicated to the United States.

## Support service levels

Critical incidents are acknowledged within two covered hours. Normal incidents are acknowledged within one covered business day. Resolution times depend on diagnosis and must not be presented as guaranteed recovery times.

## Proposal commitments

Proposals must not guarantee zero downtime, zero data loss, perfect security, or unlimited capacity. Claims must be supported by an agreed architecture, measurable acceptance criteria, assumptions, and explicit exclusions.
